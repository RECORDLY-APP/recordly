1. What Recordly Is (and Is Not)

Recordly is a bookkeeping, receipt, and contribution-tracking ledger. Recordly does not hold funds, process bank transfers, or act as a bank, escrow agent, or licensed payment processor. All money transfers occur directly between admins/sellers and buyers/members through external channels.

2. Accuracy, Trust & Audit History

  • Admins and authorized staff must log genuine payments, dates, and proofs accurately.
  • Once a payment is logged, edits and voids require a stated reason and remain visible in the payment change log so buyers can see when and why a figure changed.
  • Buyers may flag an inaccurate payment using "Report a problem."

3. Member Data & Lawful Consent

When adding member names, phone numbers, email addresses, or uploading payment screenshots, you confirm you have a legitimate purpose and permission to record and share payment progress with those individuals, and that you will not upload unlawful, abusive, or deceptive material.

4. Staff Accounts & Plan Limits

  • Workspace owners are responsible for actions taken by staff accounts they invite. Staff can log payments and add members but cannot delete groups, void payments without owner permission, or delete the account.
  • Usage quotas (Free, Pro, and Business plans) are enforced on the server and database. Attempting to circumvent limits or abuse notifications/emails is prohibited.

5. Acceptable Use & Safety

We prohibit fraud, impersonation, harassment, and spam in discoverable groups and group chats. Groups or users reported for abuse may be hidden or suspended. Users can block or report any discoverable group at any time.

6. Account Termination & Liability

You may stop using Recordly and delete your account at any time. To the fullest extent permitted by law, Recordly is provided "as is" without liability for indirect losses or disputes between group members and sellers.
