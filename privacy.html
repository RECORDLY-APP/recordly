Recordly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to all Recordly web, mobile PWA, and shared link services.

Recordly helps group administrators, associations, and small businesses record contributions, instalment schedules, sales, expenses, and debts clearly. Because you entrust us with names, contact details, and payment records, we keep data collection minimal, transparent, and under your control.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your name, email address or phone number, authentication identifiers, and your chosen business or group display name.
  • Records & Member Data: Group titles, payment targets, instalment due dates, member names, optional phone numbers and email addresses, payment amounts, dates, notes, edit/void reasons, and problem reports.
  • Payment Proofs: Transfer screenshots or receipts you intentionally attach to a payment are stored in private, access-controlled cloud storage.
  • Device Preferences: Theme choice, notification settings, Web Push subscription keys (only when you enable live alerts), and local offline cache data.

2. How We Use Information

  • To display accurate payment histories, receipts, statements, overdue alerts, and audit logs to authorized admins, staff, and buyers.
  • To send payment receipts, due-date reminders, and live payment notifications when requested by you or your group admin.
  • To prevent fraud, resolve disputed amounts, and enforce plan limits and safety reports.

3. Visibility & Search Controls

  • Private Links: Personal progress links show payment history to the link holder. Admins can hide targets and remaining balances from public visitors.
  • Search Discovery: Groups only appear in Recordly Search when the admin enables "Show in Recordly search." You can hide your own profile from search anytime via Settings → Hide me from search.
  • Blocking & Reporting: Any user can block or report a discoverable group or account.

4. Support, Safety & Administration

The Recordly team can see basic account details (your name, email address, plan, when you joined and when you last used the app) and the abuse reports users send us. We use this to give support, keep Recordly safe, review reports and fix problems. When it is needed to investigate a report or to help you with a request you made, the team may also look at the records in the affected account. We never see your password.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Payment edit and void logs are preserved while a record is active to maintain financial accountability between buyers and sellers. You may export your workspace to CSV at any time, or permanently delete your account and associated records from Settings → Leaving Recordly → Close account.
