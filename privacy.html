Privacy Policy

Recordly · Last updated: 24 October 2026

Recordly is an app for keeping simple records of contributions, sales, expenses and debts, and for sharing private progress links. It is run by Afolabi Paul ("I", "me", "Recordly"). This page explains what information Recordly collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information Recordly collects

  • Account details: your name, email address and, if you choose phone login, your phone number. If you sign in with Google, Recordly receives your name and email from Google. Recordly never sees your Google password.
  • Records you enter: group or record names, descriptions, target amounts, the names and optional phone numbers of people in a record, payments (amount, date, time, method and notes), sales, customers, expenses, debts, receipts and comments.
  • Activity needed to run the app: alerts about payments, comments, join requests and visits to your shared links. When someone opens a link, Recordly may store the time, how they arrived (shared link, invite or search), and their name if they are logged in.
  • On your device: your settings (such as dark mode and hiding money amounts), a copy of your records so the app works without internet, and your app lock settings. If you set an app PIN, only a scrambled (hashed) form of it is saved on your phone. It is never sent to our servers.

2. How the information is used

  • To create your account and keep you logged in.
  • To store your records safely and show them back to you on any device.
  • To let the people you add see their own progress through a private link.
  • To send you account emails, such as login codes and password resets.
  • To notify you about payments, comments, join requests and link visits.

Recordly does not sell your information and does not show ads.

3. Who can see your information

  • You can see all records you create.
  • People you add can see only their own payments and progress through their link. If you turn on a group table or group link, people with that link can see the group's names and totals.
  • Discover: a group appears in search only if its admin turns it on. A person's name appears in search only if they allow it, and they can turn this off. Joining a group always needs the admin's approval.
  • Payment method notes are visible only to the group admin and the person the payment belongs to.

4. Services Recordly uses

  • Supabase stores account and record data and handles login.
  • Google provides the "Continue with Google" login if you choose it.
  • Brevo sends account emails such as login codes and password resets.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's web pages.

These providers process data only to provide their service and have their own privacy policies.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access to your records is limited by your account. The app logs you out after a period of inactivity, and asks for confirmation before you reset or clear your workspace. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong password and keep your login private.

6. How long information is kept

Your information is kept while your account is active. If you ask for your account to be deleted, your account and the records linked to it will be removed, except where the law requires something to be kept.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can edit or delete any record, person, payment, sale, expense or debt inside the app.
  • You can hide your name from Discover search and turn off Discover listing for any group.
  • To delete your account and all your data, open More in the app and tap Delete my account. Full steps are on the account deletion page.
  • If you cannot log in, or you want a copy of your data, email afolabipaul003@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Recordly account deletion". Requests are handled within 30 days.

8. Important note about money

Recordly is a record-keeping tool. It does not hold, send or receive money, and it does not process payments. Payments happen outside the app, and the admin logs them.

9. Children

Recordly is not meant for children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
