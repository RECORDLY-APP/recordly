Delete your Recordly account

Recordly · Last updated: 24 October 2026

Option 1: Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open Recordly and log in.
  2. Tap More at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm with your password. If you signed in with Google, type the word CONFIRM instead.

Option 2: Ask by email

If you cannot log in, email afolabipaul003@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Recordly account deletion". Requests are handled within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login and profile (name, email, phone number).
  • All records, people, payments, sales, customers, expenses, debts, receipts and comments you created.
  • Your notifications, join requests and link-visit history.
  • The copy saved on the phone you used to delete it.

If you only joined someone else's group as a member, their group and its payment history stay with them. Your login is removed and your name stays on the group's list as a person they added.

What is kept

Nothing is kept once deletion finishes, except backups held by our hosting providers, which are removed on their normal schedule, and anything the law requires to be kept. Deleting an account cannot be undone.
